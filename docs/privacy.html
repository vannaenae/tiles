Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Tiles does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

What Tiles reads on your Mac

To arrange windows, Tiles uses the macOS Accessibility permission to read the position, size and title of open windows and the names of the apps that own them, and to move and resize those windows. This happens on your Mac only. Tiles does not record your screen, read the contents of your windows, or log your keystrokes.

What Tiles stores

Your settings are saved in a file on your Mac (~/.config/tiles/config.json). Saved window arrangements store app identifiers and layout choices only, never window titles. Removing Tiles and that folder removes everything.

Network use

Once a day Tiles asks GitHub whether a newer version exists, and downloads it from GitHub if you choose to update. GitHub sees your IP address when this happens, as with any download. You can switch this off in Tiles → General → Updates. Tiles makes no other network requests.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
